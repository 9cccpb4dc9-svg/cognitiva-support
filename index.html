Simple tools, handled with care

Cognitiva brings journals, medication lists, reminders, calendars, health notes, caregiver details, exports, and clear tutorials into one calm iPhone and iPad app.

Medical notice: Cognitiva does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, monitoring, or emergency response. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions and local emergency services during an emergency.

Privacy at a glance

  • Personal journals, medications, health entries, caregivers, and emergency contacts stay on the device.
  • Cognitiva does not sell data or use advertising trackers.
  • Public Family Feed and Forum posts use Apple CloudKit and are visible to other Cognitiva users.
  • Location is displayed locally only when enabled and is not sent to caregivers or emergency contacts.
