Be kind and protect privacy

Do not post harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content, violence, spam, impersonation, illegal content, copyrighted material without permission, or misleading medical claims.

Do not post phone numbers, email addresses, links, exact locations, passwords, private medical records, or another person’s information without permission.

Community posts are not medical advice

Personal experiences may be shared respectfully, but users must not present them as diagnosis or treatment instructions. Medication and health decisions belong with qualified healthcare professionals.

Report and block

Swipe a public post, topic, or reply to find Report and Block. Reports identify the content, reason, date, and optional explanation for moderation. Blocking hides that author’s content on the device. Blocked users can be reviewed in Settings.

Moderation

Reports may result in content removal, warnings, posting restrictions, or access restrictions depending on severity and history. Safety reports should be acknowledged within 24 hours and reviewed within 72 hours. Credible immediate threats may be referred to appropriate emergency or legal channels.

Appeals and questions

Use the GitHub support route to ask about a moderation decision. GitHub issues are public, so identify the issue without including personal health or contact information.
