Common help

  • Permissions: Open Apple Settings, select Cognitiva, and review the relevant permission.
  • Community iCloud: Confirm the device is signed into iCloud and iCloud Drive is available.
  • Notifications: Review Cognitiva notification permission and Focus settings.
  • Calendar: Confirm Full Access is enabled under Calendar privacy settings.
  • Sleep: Confirm Sleep Analysis access is enabled for Cognitiva in Apple Health.
  • Export: Generate the PDF again, choose Save to Files, then open the saved file.

Community safety

Use the Report action on a Family Feed post, forum topic, or reply. Use Block to hide an author’s content on the device. Reported content is reviewed for action under the Community Guidelines.

Response targets

Community safety reports should be acknowledged within 24 hours and reviewed within 72 hours. Credible threats of immediate harm should be handled as quickly as possible. General public support issues should receive a response within five business days.

Medical and emergency notice

Cognitiva support cannot provide medical advice or emergency help. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions and local emergency services during an emergency.
