Overview

Cognitiva is a memory, organization, and care-support application. It may handle sensitive information such as journal entries, medication lists, health notes, selected contact details, photos, audio notes, reminders, calendar information, and optional location information.

Cognitiva does not sell personal information and does not use personal or health information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Information stored on the device

Personal journals, journal media, medication lists, reminders, health entries, imported sleep duration, caregiver details, emergency contacts, and local settings are stored in the app’s protected local storage. Cognitiva does not synchronize this personal care information through iCloud.

Public community content

If a person chooses to post in the Family Feed or Forums, the author name, post text, forum topic or reply, date, and optional feed photo are stored in Apple CloudKit’s public database. This content is visible to other Cognitiva users. People should not publish private medical, contact, account, or location information.

Reports about public content are stored in CloudKit for moderation. A report contains the target record, selected reason, date, and optional short explanation. It should not contain unrelated health information.

Apple permissions

Optional features request access only when used:

  • Apple Health: read-only Sleep Analysis access after Track Sleep is enabled.
  • Calendar: access to display and create events.
  • Contacts: access to the one contact selected in Apple’s contact picker.
  • Photos and Microphone: optional journal or Family Feed media chosen by the person.
  • Location: current position displayed on this device while enabled. Cognitiva does not transmit it to caregivers or emergency contacts.
  • Notifications: local medication and routine reminders.

Permissions can be denied or changed in Apple Settings.

Medication catalog

Cognitiva downloads a medication-name catalog from the U.S. National Library of Medicine RxNav service. Searching occurs locally after the catalog is downloaded. Standard network information may be processed by that service when the catalog request is made.

This product uses publicly available data courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine, National Institutes of Health, Department of Health and Human Services. NLM is not responsible for this product and does not endorse or recommend it.

Exports and user-directed sharing

A person may create a PDF and intentionally send or save it with Apple’s share sheet. The person chooses the destination. Once information leaves Cognitiva, the recipient or destination service controls that copy.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until it is deleted in the app or the app and its local data are removed. Public content remains until its author deletes it or it is removed through moderation. Blocking an author hides that author’s community content on the device but does not delete the public records.

To request review or removal of public content, use the in-app Report action. For another privacy question, use the support route below and do not include private medical information in a public GitHub issue.

Security

Cognitiva relies on Apple platform protections, app sandboxing, device data protection, HTTPS, and CloudKit security. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect the device passcode and review destinations before exporting sensitive information.

Children

Cognitiva is not directed to children and is not intended for the App Store Kids category. Public community features are subject to the published Community Guidelines.

Medical and emergency disclaimer

Cognitiva does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, monitoring, or emergency response. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Policy changes and contact

This page will be updated when material data practices change. The effective date will be revised. Support and privacy questions are handled through the GitHub support link. GitHub issues are public, so do not include health, medication, contact, account, or location information.
